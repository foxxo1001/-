《越用越轻》Lighter With Use
把复杂留给机器，把简单留给人。

Leave complexity to machines; keep simplicity for people. Good tools should feel lighter the longer you use them — the same reason I study embedded systems and build with AI.

我相信好的工具应该越用越轻——少一层框架，就多一分专注。这是我学嵌入式、用 AI 的同一条理由。
